S2648 dataset — manual download required
=========================================
Source: Potapov et al. 2009, Protein Eng. Des. Sel. 22(9):553-560
DOI: 10.1093/protein/gzp030

Obtain the CSV from one of:
  1. Paper supplementary materials (Table S1)
  2. ThermoMutDB download page: https://biosig.lab.uq.edu.au/thermomutdb/
  3. Contact corresponding author

Expected columns: pdb_id, chain, wildtype, position, mutant, ddg
Save as: s2648.csv in this directory.
